🔔 Reminders

Notifications from this site

My Tracker sends notifications to this device, even when the site is closed:

  • 8:00 every morning: overdue tasks, tasks due today, tasks in progress.
  • Keep going: a nudge for “In progress” tasks with no update for 3+ days.
  • Your own time: set Remind me at on a task (✎ Edit task).

Calendar link

Subscribe to this private link in your calendar app. Every unfinished task with a deadline shows up on its deadline day, and overdue tasks also appear on today so you remember to continue them.

Google Calendar

  1. Copy the link above.
  2. On a computer, open Google Calendar → Add calendar → From URL, paste the link, click Add calendar.
  3. In Settings, click My Tracker (under “Other calendars”) and set All-day event notifications, e.g. “1 day before at 9:00” and “On the day at 8:00”.

iPhone / Apple Calendar

  1. Settings → Calendar → Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, paste the link.
  2. Reminders come with the calendar: 9:00 the day before and 8:00 on the deadline day.
Google refreshes subscribed calendars on its own schedule (usually every few hours up to a day), so new tasks can take a while to appear. For something urgent, open the task and use Add to Google Calendar, which adds it right away.
Anyone with this link can see your task titles and deadlines. If it leaks, click New link: the old link stops working and you subscribe again with the new one.
